Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Spendly is a personal expense tracking application, and we aim to collect only what's needed to provide that service.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with account activity like login timestamps needed to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the core features of Spendly: logging your expenses, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We may also use aggregated, anonymized data to understand how the service is used and to improve it. We do not use your personal expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You can edit or delete individual expenses at any time within the app. If you delete your account, your associated data is permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required to comply with legal obligations.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party service providers for functions such as hosting or infrastructure, who are only given access to the data necessary to perform those functions and are required to protect it. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
